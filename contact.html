Before we talk.

What information should I prepare?

A location, approximate project size, intended use, reference images, and a rough timeline are helpful. It is also fine to start with just an idea.

Can I contact you before I own land?

Yes. Early conversations can help clarify site needs, project scope, and the right next step.

Do you work outside Lombok?

Project locations are reviewed individually. Share the location and brief with our team so we can discuss feasibility.
