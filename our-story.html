A study in bamboo form · Lombok

A MATERIAL WITH CHARACTER

Strong in structure.
Gentle in presence.

Bamboo asks us to design with its natural curves, rhythm, and scale in mind. We start by understanding the site and the people who will use a space, then shape each decision around that context.

01

Listen to the place

Sun, wind, views, and daily routines are part of the brief. The best starting point is a careful reading of the land.

02

Design with the material

We explore spans, joints, and roof forms early, using drawings and 3D studies to make the idea easier to understand.

03

Make with care

Material preparation, skilled hands, and coordination between workshop and site help turn a concept into a lasting place.

FROM FIRST THOUGHT TO BUILT SPACE

A considered process, at every scale.

Every project has its own conditions. These shared principles keep the work connected from the first conversation to the final details.

01

Understand

Talk through the brief, budget, site, and the experience you want to create.

02

Imagine

Explore spatial ideas and material direction through sketches, plans, and 3D views.

03

Resolve

Coordinate structure, details, and construction planning before work moves to site.

04

Realize

Build, review, and refine the work so the finished space feels at home in its setting.
